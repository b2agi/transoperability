# What Is Transoperability?

**Transoperability is the property of one domain's operation changing the operation of another domain across borders — nations, industries, and disciplines.**

Interoperability connects systems. Transoperability transfers effects.

In an era where intelligence becomes physical, a decision in one domain does not stay in that domain. An AI model's performance changes how capital is allocated. A capital structure changes who controls a technology. Control changes security and supply-chain exposure. Security and trust change how nations cooperate, and cooperation decides which technology enters which market. Each step crosses a border. Each step changes the next domain's operation.

We call that transfer Transoperability.

---

## Interoperability vs. Transoperability

| | Interoperability | Transoperability |
|---|---|---|
| Question | Can the systems work together? | Does one domain's operation change another's? |
| Level | Technical — systems, data, protocols, standards | Cross-domain — nations, industries, disciplines, capital, security |
| Mechanism | Connection, interface, exchange | Effect transfer, consequence, changed judgment |
| Verification | Can it communicate? Can it exchange data? | Does the effect change the next domain's decision and outcome? |
| Example | A robot from Company A works with a cloud from Company B | An AI deployment changes capital allocation, which changes control, which changes security exposure, which changes international cooperation |

Interoperability is necessary but not sufficient. Two systems can interoperate perfectly while their industries, investors, and regulators make decisions as if the other domains did not exist. The gap between technical interoperability and judgment interoperability is where Transoperability lives.

---

## Transoperability and EPAI

EPAI — Embodied Physical Agentic Intelligence — is the framework for seeing the ecosystem: intelligence, people, machines, infrastructure, capital, institutions, and the lives they affect, as one connected system.

Transoperability is the observable property of that ecosystem in motion.

- EPAI is the lens: **how** to see the connections across domains.
- Transoperability is the behavior: **what** happens when a change in one domain crosses into another.

They are not competing terms. EPAI explains the ecosystem. Transoperability names the transfer that makes the ecosystem matter.

---

## Why now — and the space it comes from

At the UN ITU-T working group session in Hangzhou (WG5: Connectivity & Interoperability), interoperability was discussed at the technical layer — how clouds, networks, AI systems, and robots connect and work together across vendors and nations.

That layer is necessary. But the transfer that follows — from technical capability to capital allocation, from ownership to control, from control to security, from security to international cooperation — sits **outside the mandate of any single standards body**. Standards bodies set rules for systems. No one body sets rules for how the effects of those systems cross every border at once.

That unmapped space is Transoperability.

---

## What Transoperability is not

- It is not a formally adopted international standard.
- It is not a certification, a rating, or a score.
- It is not a replacement for interoperability — interoperability remains the technical foundation.
- It is not a claim that every cross-domain effect is deterministic or predictable.
- It is not a license to conflate correlation with causation.

It is a research concept and a decision lens: when a change crosses a border — national, industrial, or disciplinary — trace what changes on the other side, and who decides with what evidence.

---

## The core sentence

> Interoperability connects systems. Transoperability transfers effects. EPAI helps you see both before you decide.

# 한글판 요약 — Transoperability(트랜스오퍼러빌리티)

**Transoperability는 한 영역의 작동이 국경·산업·분야를 넘어 다른 영역의 작동을 바꾸는 성질이다.**

Interoperability(상호운용성)는 시스템이 연결되어 함께 작동하는 능력이다. Transoperability는 그 효과가 경계를 넘어 전이되는 것이다.

AI 모델의 성능은 자본 배분을 바꾸고, 자본 구조는 기술의 통제권을 바꾸고, 통제권은 안보·공급망 노출을 바꾸고, 안보와 신뢰는 국가 간 협력을 바꾼다. 각 단계는 경계를 건너고, 각 단계는 다음 영역의 작동을 바꾼다. 그 전이를 우리는 Transoperability라고 부른다.

- **EPAI는 렌즈다** — 생태계의 연결을 어떻게 볼 것인가.
- **Transoperability는 작동 방식이다** — 한 영역의 변화가 다른 영역에 들어갈 때 무슨 일이 일어나는가.

항저우 ITU-T WG5(Connectivity & Interoperability)에서 논의된 상호운용성은 기술 계층의 문제였다. 그 계층에서 시작된 전이 — 기술 역량 → 자본 배분 → 소유·통제 → 안보 → 국제협력 — 는 어떤 단일 표준기구의 권한 밖에 있다. 그 지도에 없는 공간이 Transoperability다.

**Transoperability는 국제 표준이 아니다. 인증·등급·점수가 아니다. 상호운용성을 대체하지 않는다. 상관관계를 인과로 오인하게 하는 도구가 아니다.** 경계를 넘는 변화가 일어날 때, 반대편에서 무엇이 바뀌는지, 누가 어떤 근거로 결정하는지를 추적하는 연구 개념이자 결정 렌즈다.

> Interoperability는 시스템을 연결한다. Transoperability는 효과를 전이한다. EPAI는 결정하기 전에 둘 다 보게 한다.

# 中文版摘要 — Transoperability（跨效运性）

**Transoperability（跨效运性）是指一个领域的作用跨越国境、行业与学科边界，改变另一个领域运作方式的属性。**

Interoperability（互操作性）是系统连接并协同工作的能力。Transoperability 是效果跨越边界发生转移的过程。

AI 模型的性能改变资本配置，资本结构改变技术控制权，控制权改变安全与供应链暴露，安全与信任改变国家间合作，而合作又决定技术进入哪个市场、资本投向哪里。每一步都在跨越边界，每一步都在改变下一个领域的运作。我们将这种转移称为 Transoperability。

- **EPAI 是透镜** —— 如何看见生态系统中的连接。
- **Transoperability 是运作方式** —— 一个领域的变化进入另一个领域时会发生什么。

在杭州 ITU-T WG5（连接与互操作性）会议上讨论的互操作性属于技术层。但技术层之上发生的转移——从技术能力到资本配置，从所有权到控制权，从控制到安全，从安全到国际合作——处于任何单一标准机构的职权之外。这块未被绘制的地图，就是 Transoperability。

**Transoperability 不是正式采用的国际标准，不是认证、评级或评分，不替代互操作性，也不允许把相关性误当作因果。** 它是一个研究概念与决策透镜：当变化跨越边界时，追踪另一侧发生了什么改变，以及谁依据什么证据在做决定。

> Interoperability 连接系统。Transoperability 转移效果。EPAI 帮助你在决定之前看见两者。
